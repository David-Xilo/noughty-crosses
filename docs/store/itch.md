## Description

**Noughty Crosses: every clever way to play noughts and crosses, on one lamp-lit game table.**

Open the cabinet and pick a board from its drawers. Classic. Vanishing, where your oldest
mark fades away. Misère, where three in a row loses. Notakto, Big board, Gravity,
Ultimate, Overtime, Misère wild, Three men's morris, Saucer, Order and Chaos, and
Nesting, where pieces stack by size. Each mode has a one-line rule card and a ten-second
demo, and each says plainly whether perfect play draws or who has the edge.

Play a friend on the same device, or the computer at three levels: Casual blocks your
obvious line and misses your fork, Keen rarely slips, Sharp never does. Most games take
one to three minutes.

- 13 modes, all with exact rules and honest fairness labels
- A 3D table with crafted wood and brass, or a plain 2D board for any device
- Keyboard, controller, mouse and touch; a game saves after every move
- High contrast, text size up to 200%, reduced motion, no timers
- Free. No ads, no accounts, no analytics

The code and the assets' sources and licences are on GitHub:
https://github.com/David-Xilo/noughty-crosses. Problems and ideas go to the issues page there.
